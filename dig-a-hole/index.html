Tips: how to come out on land

This is hard! About 71% of Earth is covered by water. Only about 1 out of every 7 pieces of land has land on the other side.

Try these spots that work:

  • Argentina or Chile comes out in China.
  • The middle of Spain (near Madrid) comes out in New Zealand.
  • Hawaii comes out in Botswana, in Africa.
  • Ecuador comes out in Sumatra, Indonesia.
  • Peru comes out in Cambodia or Thailand.
  • Greenland or northern Canada comes out in Antarctica.

Strategies:

  • Avoid the Pacific's opposite. The Pacific Ocean is so big that most of Africa, Europe, India and the USA come out in the water.
  • Use Antarctica. It is a big target. Dig near the North Pole and you will often land on it.
  • Think upside down. Find a big piece of land, then dig on the opposite side of the globe, on the other side of the equator.
